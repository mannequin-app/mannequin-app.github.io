Mannequin

Privacy Policy

Effective 8 November 2026 · Support: mannequin_app@icloud.com

Mannequin doesn't collect any data. There are no accounts, sign-ins, analytics, ads or tracking. The only server the app talks to is its own file server, which sends the app the data it needs and keeps no record of who downloads it.

What the app handles

WhatWhere it goesWhere it's kept
The app's data about 3.5 GB Downloaded from the app's file server when you set the app up, then processed on the device. Each time the app opens, it asks the server whether there's an update, and downloads only what changed if you accept it. On this device, until you delete the app.
Your IP address Seen by the file server, as by any website, so it can send the files back. The app sends the server nothing else about you: no account, name or device ID. Nowhere. The file server keeps no request logs.
Photos of your loadout Saved to your photo library when you take one. The app asks only to add photos and can't see the others. Your photo library.
Saved loadouts and settings Nowhere. On this device.
Loadout codes Made and read on the device. They go only where you paste or send them yourself. Wherever you put them.

The file server

The app downloads from mannequin-files.mannequin-app.workers.dev, which runs on Cloudflare. The server only sends files, and its request logs are turned off. Like any web host, Cloudflare sees your IP address while it delivers the files and protects its network. What Cloudflare does with that is covered by Cloudflare's privacy policy.

The app doesn't sign in to Steam or connect to it. Mannequin is not made by Valve and is not affiliated with Valve Corporation.

No tracking

Mannequin has no analytics, no ads and no third-party code that collects data. It doesn't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

If you chose to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may pass crash reports and usage numbers on to the developer. That's Apple's feature, covered by Apple's privacy policy, and you can turn it off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Deleting your data

  • Deleting the app deletes everything it downloaded and made: its data, saved loadouts and settings. Photos you saved stay in your photo library.
  • The developer holds no data about you, so there's nothing to ask the developer to delete.

Children

Mannequin doesn't collect anything from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a version of the app ever handles data differently, this page will be updated with a new date before that version comes out.
