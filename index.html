Mannequin

Privacy Policy

Effective 3 November 2026 · Support: mannequin_app@icloud.com

Mannequin doesn't collect any data. The developer runs no servers and never receives anything from the app. There are no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking. The only service the app talks to is Valve's Steam.

What the app handles

WhatWhere it goesWhere it's kept
Your Steam account name and password Straight to Steam, to sign you in. If you sign in with a QR code instead, you approve it in the Steam Mobile app and Mannequin never sees your password. The password isn't kept. The account name is kept in this device's Keychain.
Your Steam sign-in token Steam issues it when you sign in. The app sends it back only to Steam. This device's Keychain. It isn't synced to iCloud or moved to a new device, and signing out deletes it.
Your Steam nickname Read from Steam and shown in Settings. On this device. Signing out deletes it.
TF2 game files about 3.2 GB Downloaded from Steam's content servers, then converted on the device. On this device, until you delete the app.
Your TF2 items and loadouts only when you import them Read from Steam and TF2's item server with your sign-in. The loadouts you import are saved on this device.
Photos of your loadout Saved to your photo library when you take one. The app asks only to add photos and can't see the others. Your photo library.
Saved loadouts and settings Nowhere. On this device.
Loadout codes Made on the device. They go only where you paste or send them yourself. Wherever you put them.

Steam

Mannequin is not made by Valve and is not affiliated with Valve Corporation. When you sign in, download game files or import loadouts, the app connects directly to Valve's servers: api.steampowered.com, Steam's connection servers and Steam's content servers. What Valve does with that is covered by Valve's privacy policy.

While the app imports a loadout, Steam shows you as playing TF2 for a few seconds. Your Steam friends may see that, and a TF2 game running on another computer on your account is disconnected.

No tracking

Mannequin has no analytics, no ads and no third-party code that collects data. It doesn't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

If you chose to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may pass crash reports and usage numbers on to the developer. That's Apple's feature, covered by Apple's privacy policy, and you can turn it off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Deleting your data

  • Sign out in Mannequin's Settings to delete the sign-in token and nickname from the device.
  • You can also end the app's sign-in from Steam, for example with Deauthorize all other devices in your Steam Guard settings.
  • Deleting the app deletes the game files, saved loadouts and settings. Sign out first, because iOS can keep Keychain items after an app is deleted.
  • The developer holds no data about you, so there's nothing to ask the developer to delete.

Children

Mannequin doesn't collect anything from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a version of the app ever handles data differently, this page will be updated with a new date before that version comes out.
